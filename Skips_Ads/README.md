#Skips_Ads

I always play youtube music videos in a loop and do my work at side. It has been good, but recently youtube has come u with this long advertisment, which require you to skip the ads after 30 secs. If you miss to skip, it goes on playing the entire advertisment ,which sometimes disturbs the mood of the song. 
<p> So this .py script when run at the background, detects the skip ad button and automatically clicks it. Thus we can skip the advertisment just after 30 secs </p>
<p> I used pyautogui library and opencv library to capture the screen and I find the skip ad button from the youtube and make a click on those co-ordinates by using the pyautogui library</p>
